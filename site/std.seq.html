    Looking in a slice and cutting it (spec 05): methods of every [T], and so of every [n]T, whose items have ==, text among them, by an impl without an interface.

  • std.seq.sort

    Ordering a slice in place (spec 05): methods of every [T], and so of every [n]T, with no allocator. Short runs are insertion sorted; longer ones are heap sorted, so no input is slow. The sort is not stable: items that are equal may change places.
