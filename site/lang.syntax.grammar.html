lang.syntax.grammar

The grammar of Vun, defined once with nodes.vun, which holds the kinds of nodes and their productions, and tables.vun, the tables that say how they nest: its tokens and tables. Generates the Rust vun-grammar crate, lang.syntax, the formatter's tables and the editor grammars (spec 13, section 5).

cargo gen reads both files as literal data. Into Rust it translates each declaration by shape, never by name:

  • a PascalCase record of lists defines a kind: its entries are the kind's variants, and each list is also a table of its own. An entry is .name, a spelling "text" (named after itself), .{ .name = .x, .text = "..." }, or .name(terms...) for a kind with a production (see NodeKind), whose fields and their counts, variants of Count, it derives;
  • an enum declaration defines an enum;
  • any other list is a table; .name items resolve to the one kind or enum that has them all.

What it interprets, the productions and the tables editors and the parser share (parts, admits, precedence, ...), it reads by type, and resolves every name and path in them; what editors make of the grammar is in editors.vun.

value BorrowWord

#[lint.allow(std.lints.naming.case, "the grammar generator makes a Rust enum of it")]
BorrowWord := .{ .qualifiers = ["temp"], .modes = ["mut", "out", "take", "static"], }

The words after & naming a borrow mode (appendix B, the table borrows): first a qualifier of how long a call holds the borrow, then a word of what it does to the place. mut is a keyword; the others are words only there.
