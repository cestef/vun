std.hash

Hashing: a value feeds its parts to a Hasher, which answers a u64. Values that are equal (lang.ops.Eq) hash equal. A struct or an enum whose parts can be hashed can be hashed without an impl, by derive_hash; Fnv is a hasher for tables.

Namespaces

  • std.hash.adler32

    Adler-32 (zlib's checksum): two sums modulo 65521, of the bytes and of the first sum after each byte. Faster than a CRC and weaker on short inputs.

  • std.hash.crc32

    CRC-32 (the checksum of Ethernet, zlib and PNG): the remainder of the bytes as a polynomial, reflected, with the polynomial 0xEDB88320. It is a checksum against accidents, not a hash against an adversary.

  • std.hash.tests

    Tests of std.hash: equal values hash equal, different ones almost never do, and types hash by their parts without an impl.
