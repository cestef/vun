lang.bounds

What bounds hold (spec 04): the iterator of lang.Bounds in lang.ops reads them.

function empty

empty: ($T: type, b: Bounds(T)) -> bool = _

Whether b holds no item, such as 3..3.

function first

first: ($T: type, b: Bounds(T)) -> T = _

The least item of b, which is not empty.

function after

after: ($T: type, b: Bounds(T), x: T) -> T = _

The item after x in b, or x itself when it is the greatest: counting up never leaves T.
