std.time

Time as the system keeps it (spec 08, section 2): spans of time, and readings of the clocks a hosted program reaches through its std.os.System.

enum Clock

Clock := enum { ... }

A clock the system keeps.

variant monotonic

monotonic

Never goes back; its readings are measured from an instant the system chose, such as when it booted, and mean nothing across processes.

variant real

real

The time of day, since 1970-01-01 00:00 UTC; it moves when the system's time is set.
