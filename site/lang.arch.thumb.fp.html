lang.arch.thumb.fp

The floating-point extension: arithmetic, comparisons, moves, loads and stores, and conversions, single precision and double (Armv7-M FPv5, the encodings of VFPv4). Where a form is defined at both precisions, its double-precision one sets bit 8 (sz).

value binary

binary := _t.Family{ .forms = &[.{ .base = .\or(0xee000a00), .sizes = _float, .args = &[_d_w, _n, _m], .requires = _single }], }

Arithmetic of two operands.
