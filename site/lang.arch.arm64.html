lang.arch.arm64

64-bit ARM (spec 07, section 6): its registers, and each instruction with its forms as the toolchain's assembler encodes them (Arm A64 ISA), with what each form reads and writes. The instructions are declared in control, fp, int and memory, what their operand positions take in operand; vun-isa-arm64's tables are generated from these declarations. An alias is an instruction of its own, its fixed fields in its base word: mov is orr from the zero register, cmp is subs into it.

Namespaces

  • lang.arch.arm64.control

    Branches, calls, traps, barriers and page addresses (Arm A64 ISA, branches, exception generation and system instructions).

  • lang.arch.arm64.fp

    Scalar floating-point instructions (Arm A64 ISA, scalar floating-point data processing): the instruction's width is the precision, or for a conversion the integer's width.

  • lang.arch.arm64.int

    Integer moves, arithmetic, logic, shifts, extensions and conditional selects (Arm A64 ISA, data processing).

  • lang.arch.arm64.memory

    Loads, stores and atomics (Arm A64 ISA, loads and stores). A single access has a form per addressing mode, tried in order: a scaled offset, an unscaled one (ldur), a register index, pre- and post-index, then the low bits of a symbol's address.

  • lang.arch.arm64.operand

    What an operand position of an arm64 form takes, and how its operand is encoded: registers by width, immediates, addressing modes, conditions and branch targets.
