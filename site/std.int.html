std.int

Integer methods (spec 04, section 3): narrowing to another integer type, an impl for every Int(r) over the primitives of lang.int.

Namespaces

  • std.int.bits

    Integers as the bits of their storage (spec 04, rule 8): rotating them and counting them. A signed value is its two's complement, so every Int(r) is rotated and counted by its word.

  • std.int.bytes

    Integers as the bytes of their storage (spec 04, rule 7): to_le and to_be give the bytes of a value, from_le and from_be read a value from them. A signed value is its two's complement, so every Int(r) is read and written alike.

  • std.int.tests

    Tests of std.int: integers read from text.
