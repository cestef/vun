std.codec

Codecs: bytes as text in the encodings of RFC 4648 (Base: hex, base32, base64) and as variable-length integers (varint). Encoders write to any std.fmt.Writer and decoders write the bytes to one, so none of them needs an allocator. Checksums are std.hash.crc32 and std.hash.adler32.

Namespaces

  • std.codec.base

    Encodings that cut bytes into groups of 4, 5 or 6 bits (RFC 4648), each group a symbol of an alphabet that is a power of two long. The three are one rule with a table: the group size, the alphabet, whether a block of symbols is padded to its full size, and whether letters of either case decode.

  • std.codec.tests

    Tests of std.codec: the vectors of RFC 4648, strictness of decoding, varints.

  • std.codec.varint

    Variable-length integers (LEB128): seven bits of the number to a byte, least significant first, the high bit of a byte set when another follows. A signed number goes by its zigzag, which keeps small magnitudes short.
