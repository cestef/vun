lang.float

Floating-point operations that are explicit about precision (spec 04, section 2).

Namespaces

  • lang.float.ops

    Floats' operators (spec 02, section 4): the impls of lang.ops for every float type, over the constructor lang.Float, as the primitives of lang.float.

  • lang.float.soft

    Float operations no instruction computes, written with those that do (spec 13, section 3): each takes its operands' encodings apart through lang.float.bits, computes on integers, and assembles the result as IEEE 754 gives it. lang.float.rem and mul_add run as these.
