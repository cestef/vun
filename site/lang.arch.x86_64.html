lang.arch.x86_64

x86-64 (spec 07, section 6): its registers, and each instruction with its forms as the toolchain's assembler encodes them (Intel SDM volume 2), with what each form reads and writes. The instructions are declared in arith, control, int and sse; vun-isa-x86_64's tables are generated from these declarations, so the language, the assembler and its listings share one table. names holds what a block of instructions is written with.

Namespaces

  • lang.arch.x86_64.arith

    Integer arithmetic, logic, comparisons and shifts; each writes the flags.

  • lang.arch.x86_64.control

    Conditions, jumps, calls, the stack, and atomic and string operations.

  • lang.arch.x86_64.int

    Integer moves and arithmetic.

  • lang.arch.x86_64.sse

    SSE instructions: moves between general and SSE registers, scalar float arithmetic, comparisons and conversions. Each has its mandatory prefix before REX, then 0F and its opcode.
