# Identity

Adopted so far (candidates live in `explore/`):

- **Color.** Pastel. Blush (the dusty rose crab) is the brand color, quartz (the rose-grey shell)
  its neutral, peach the fleck; warm stone carries surfaces and text. Rose marks errors, butter
  warnings, sea notes, sky help.
  Diagnostics renderers and syntax themes take their colors from a theme's `diag` and `code`
  roles.
- **Type.** Undecided; `tokens/type.toml` still names Bricolage Grotesque, IBM Plex Sans and
  IBM Plex Mono. Candidates are in the gallery's T section and the tweaker.
- **Name.** Always lowercase `vun`. The wordmark is a ligature: the `v` runs into the `u`, and
  the `u` and `n` share a stem. The `v` takes the accent.
- **Logo.** `logo/mark` (the mascot, square), `logo/wordmark`, `logo/lockup` (both).
- **Voice.** Plain, exact, unhurried; nothing behind the scenes.

`sample.toml` is the code specimen used on identity pages.
