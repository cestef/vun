# 94. Place states are checked on MIR

Status: accepted
Where: 05, 13

Place states are checked on MIR (`vun-mir-passes`, query `PlaceStates`): locals start `uninit` and
parameters `init`, a join of `init` and `uninit` is ambiguous, parts named statically are tracked on
their own, and a borrow parameter's target starts in the state its mode needs and must reach the
state it leaves, both columns of the table `borrows` (`place.read_uninit`, `place.ambiguous_join`,
`place.out_unwritten`). Moves, kinds and `place.linear_leak` wait for kinds; a stage declares
whether it reads MIR, and the corpus treats its missing reports in bodies MIR does not lower as
pending
