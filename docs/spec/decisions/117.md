Status: accepted
Where: 02, 12

`std.build`: `Build` records a plan of steps (`Step`, `Dep`, `Program`); `dep($name: lang.meta.Name,
params)`, `program(options)`, `flash(program, Flash)`, `test_on(program, Runner)`, `lints(levels)`,
the package-specific arguments records with inferred types; `Package` requires `build` only,
`package` being read without evaluation. `.name` where a `lang.meta.Name` is expected is the name.

that no instantiation binds is `TyKind::Param` in its generic body, whose members are the
interface's requirements for it (`Env::abstracted`), so `types.no_member` holds there too
