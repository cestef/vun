# 175. `f16` is a LIR float like the others

Status: accepted
Where: 13

`f16` is a LIR float like the others; a backend declares whether it computes it
(`BackendCaps::half`: `Computed`, or `Promoted`) and legalization holds a promoted `f16` as its
`i16` bits, every operation computed in `f32` between an exact widening and one rounding to nearest
even (correct since 24 ≥ 2 · 11 + 2), comparisons on the widened values; a promoted `f16` under the
