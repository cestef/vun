# 211. After promotion, lowering threads jumps

Status: accepted
Where: 13

its predecessor passes (a comparison of a parameter with a constant, then a branch, nothing it
defines read outside it) and through blocks that only jump; blocks left unreached stay for backends
to leave out. The backend test programs lose a further 5 to 19 percent of their native instructions
