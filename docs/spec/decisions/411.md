# 411. Microsoft x64 is a convention as data

Status: accepted
Where: 13

Microsoft x64 is a convention as data (`lang.target.Convention.win64`, `Rules::WIN64`, the registers
`vun_asm_x86_64::conventions::WIN64`): the classes count together by position (`Slots::Shared`), the
stack starts after 32 bytes of shadow space (`Rules::shadow`), a record of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bytes
travels whole in a general register whatever its fields and any other by reference
(`Records::Whole`, `Large::Address`), a float among variadic arguments travels as the integer of its
bits in the register of its position (`Variadic::Integer`), and no C integer is wider than a
register (`Rules::integer_limbs`, which was the constant 2). Its scalar placements are checked
against clang for `x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` with those of the other conventions, its records and
results by unit tests written from clang's output; the native backend does not build it yet
(`Rules::of` gives it, the backend's `backend.lacks` refuses the format)
