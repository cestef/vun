# 257. The native backends share a register allocator, `vun-regalloc`

Status: accepted
Where: 13

The native backends share a register allocator, `vun-regalloc`: linear scan on SSA after Wimmer and
Franz (lifetime intervals with holes over `Order`, splitting and spilling, preferences for
call-preserved registers and move targets, edges resolved as parallel moves), from constraints given
as data (`Machine` per architecture, `Needs` per instruction from snippets and `vun-abi`
placements), producing a plan the single-pass framework follows, and checked by a simulating checker
in tests and fuzzing
