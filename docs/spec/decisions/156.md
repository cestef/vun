# 156. Comparisons narrow

Status: accepted
Where: 04, 06, 13, appendix B

Comparisons narrow (decision 35, implemented): the table `comparisons` (`lang.syntax.desugar`, enum
`Comparison`) gives what `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `==`, `!=` tell of integer operands; an immutable
local compared is narrowed in `if` branches, `for` bodies, match arms by their guards, the right
operand of `and` (where the left holds) and `or` (where it fails), through `!`, and after an `if`
whose one branch cannot fall through; `!=` narrows only a bound; a narrowing that leaves no value is
not made. A `for` loop nothing leaves has the empty type `lang.never`. A borrowed array is iterated
as its slice (`for x in &arr` gives `&T`). `lang.ops`' slice and array iterators count through an
immutable binding narrowed by their test, so no counter needs a wider type; the bounds iterator
steps with the new total primitive `lang.bounds.after(T, b, x)` (the next item, or `x` at the end).
`Bounds(T)` at run time is its low bound, a second bound and a byte telling how it ends (none,
before the second bound, at it), so `0..=255` of `u8` fits its storage. Frame layouts come from
lowering through `vun_sema::layout::Framers` (installed by the driver like the interpreter); frame
types carry the `$for` bindings of their coroutine function
