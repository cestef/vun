# 424. `#[lang.lint.allow(lint, reason)]` may be written on a local binding as on a

Status: accepted
Where: 10

`#[lang.lint.allow(lint, reason)]` may be written on a local binding as on a declaration, and on any
expression: the lints it names are silenced for the local (or the expression) and for the calls in
its value, and a report proposes the attribute on the local's own declaration. The lint walk carries
the lints allowed at each node, entering and leaving the scopes of declarations, locals and
attributed expressions
