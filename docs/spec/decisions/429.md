# 429. A `#[layout.c]` field may be a function of `#[abi.naked]` as of `#[abi.c]`

Status: accepted
Where: 5, 7

A `#[layout.c]` field may be a function of `#[abi.naked]` as of `#[abi.c]` (`Abi::is_enterable`,
`Signature::is_enterable`): a naked function is entered by what the target defines at its address,
which is what a table of vectors holds, and its body names the convention it takes. The `rp2350`
board's table holds naked handlers, each interrupt's entering through `push(r4, lr)`, `bl`, `pop(r4,
pc)`, and its faults' reading the frame the core pushed
