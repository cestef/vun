# 262. The formatter changes whitespace only and breaks lines past a width

Status: accepted
Where: 12, 13

The formatter changes whitespace only and breaks lines past a width (`--width`, `VUN_FMT_WIDTH`, 100
by default): spaces within a line come from the grammar's tables (`Spacing` of `delimiters` inside
and of `infix` levels around, `separators`, prefix and postfix operators) and the CST's roles,
indentation from delimiter groups and list entries, comments keep their place. `vun fmt [--check]
<files>` at the root
