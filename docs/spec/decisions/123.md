# 123. `&static T` is the row `static` of `borrows`

Status: accepted
Where: 01, 02, 05

`&static T` is the row `static` of `borrows` (`lang.mem.RefStatic`, extent `static`): free, accepted
wherever a borrow of its transition and access is, and the only extent required where written;
borrows meet in the shorter extent; a string literal has type `&static [N]u8`; tuple items are
members `t.0` and `t.$(i)` with `i` known; `xs[a..b]` through a borrow lowers to a borrow of that
run of items; a type's impls are also looked up in the package declaring it
