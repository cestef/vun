# 323. What the generator interprets of the grammar, the productions and the tables

Status: accepted
Where: 13

What the generator interprets of the grammar, the productions and the tables the parser and editors
share, it reads by type, never by a declaration's shape or a name matched as text:
`#[derive(FromLiteral)]` (`vun-literal-derive`, trait in `vun_syntax::literal`) reads a literal as a
Rust type (a record as a struct, `.name` and `.name(a, b)` as an enum's variants,
`#[literal(rest)]`, `#[literal(untagged)]`, `Mismatch` naming where and what), and the grammar is
resolved once into a model (`vun-generate`'s `grammar::model`) naming tokens, kinds and fields by
typed ids, which the Rust productions, the Sublime syntax, the tree-sitter grammar, the queries and
the editor packages all write from. Productions name tables by paths to their declarations
(`.token(grammar.infix)`, `.of(.type, grammar.literal_types)`) through an alias of a file's
namespace (`grammar := lang.syntax.grammar`), which name resolution checks. Editor facts move from
the generator into `lang/syntax/editors.vun` (captures by kind, table and place, outline, text
objects, Sublime scopes, what tree-sitter implies). The table `labeled_bodies` says that a label
names a loop or a block. Record fields may carry doc comments, which the literal reader keeps.
`transparent` rows name the tokens making a kind transparent (`prefix` under `$` and `unsafe`), and
the parser reads `unterminated`, `transparent`, `labeled_bodies`, `operand_contexts` and
`member_names` (a member named by `.$` of another kind is an error holding it) through the generated
`NodeKind::starts`; the CST verifier exempts, besides recovery's own shapes, a node holding one,
whose extent recovery decided. The parser also reads `precedence` (an operand is read at a `Level`;
the forms continuing an operand are found from the productions, told apart by their leading tokens),
and `preferred`, `parts`, `admits` and `holds` where it chooses a group's kind after its children:
the first kind starting with the group's token, the preferred first, whose production they spell in
fields admitting them; `preferred` gains `closure` over `array_type`. An attribute on an operand
holds it at the attribute's level, the loosest.
