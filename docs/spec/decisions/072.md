# 72. Compound assignments are data

Status: accepted
Where: 03, 04, appendix B
Note: on the agent's recommendation

Compound assignments are data: the table `compound` of `lang.syntax.desugar` gives each `op=`'s
operator and narrowing (`to`, `wrap`, `sat`). A compile-time call's step budget covers its own steps
and the memoized steps of the calls it makes, so a call's outcome is a function of the call alone;
the budget is 2^24 unless `#[comptime.fuel(n)]` on the declaration of the function called sets
another (revised from a budget per evaluation root, which a memoized call cannot honor, and from a
budget of a call's own steps, which left chains of calls unbounded); `comptime` joins the attribute
namespaces of `std.prelude`
