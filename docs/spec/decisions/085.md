# 85. `$for` and `$if` among statements generate statements

Status: accepted
Where: 03

`$for` and `$if` among statements generate statements: a `$for` step is typed and lowered with its
binder bound, its types kept per step (`TypedBody::steps`); a `$for` is never a block's value; `$if`
as a value (last in a block, or in an expression) has the value of the branch its known conditions
select, `else if` included; a step's binder has the type of its item alone
