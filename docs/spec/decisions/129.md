# 129. Runtimes export `std.sync.enter`/`leave` and `std.task.sleep`

Status: accepted
Where: 07, 08

Runtimes export `std.sync.enter`/`leave` and `std.task.sleep`: the C-library one blocks signals and
takes a lock owned by `pthread_self()`, nesting within a thread, and sleeps by `sched_yield`; the
stm32f4 one saves `PRIMASK` and sleeps by `wfi`; `Mask` records whether its `enter` took the
section; an export is checked against every declaration of its symbol the file sees
(`link.export_mismatch`)
