# 202. The LIR builder folds integer operations, comparisons and conversions of

Status: accepted
Where: 13

The LIR builder folds integer operations, comparisons and conversions of constants within their
domain (`BinOp::fold`, `Cond::fold`, `CastOp::fold`) and selects on constants; floats are not
folded, since a fold would have to reproduce the machine's NaNs. A jump writes a parameter held in
its own register directly rather than through the carrier. `half.vun` (promoted `f16` arithmetic)
loses 13 percent more of its instructions
