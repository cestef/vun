# 93. Layout as spec 05 section 6 states it, computed by the query `Layouts`

Status: accepted
Where: 05

Layout as spec 05 section 6 states it, computed by the query `Layouts` (now `Shapes`, decision 145):
integers in their storage with the patterns past their range unused, pointers and borrows one word
(a borrow of a slice two) and never null, structs and tuples in order with padding, enums tagged by
the smallest integer holding their variants, or in the payload's unused patterns by the niche rule;
`#[layout.bits(uN)]`, `#[layout.tag(uN)]`, `#[layout.align(n)]` read as data (`Env::attribute`);
`std.meta.size` over `lang.meta.size`; a `_` field takes its space and has no name;
`layout.bits_width` when a bit struct's fields do not take its N bits. `#[layout.offset]`,
`#[layout.c]` beyond natural order, and frames have no layout yet
