# 280. `vun-abi` places 32-bit Arm's conventions through the same `Rules` as the others

Status: accepted
Where: 13

`vun-abi` places 32-bit Arm's conventions through the same `Rules` as the others: a convention
states its word and stack alignment, the largest record returned in registers, where floats travel
(`Floats::Next`, `Core` for a soft-float EABI, `Backfill` for the VFP variant, whose floats fill
back singles a double's alignment skipped), and whether a record may straddle the last general
registers and the stack (`Split::WhileStackEmpty`, AAPCS C.5), placed as `Loc::Split { parts, rest
}`; `Rules::AAPCS` and `Rules::AAPCS_VFP` are built, the soft and hard float EABIs being two
conventions since the float ABI is not the FPU's presence (`softfp`), and targets name them
`lang.target.Convention.aapcs_vfp` (`bare.cortex_m4f`) and `.aapcs` (`bare.cortex_m3`). A LIR value
never spans registers: a scalar wider than a register of its class is `Unpassable::Wider`,
legalization splitting it first (`Rules::fits`), and a wide result is one `AbiType::Wide` written
through the caller's place, returned in consecutive result registers under C; homogeneous aggregates
travel in floating-point registers whatever their size (AAPCS64 with three or four doubles
included). Checked against clang for `thumbv7em-none-eabihf` and `thumbv7m-none-eabi`
