# 174. Operations no instruction computes are Vun

Status: accepted
Where: 04, 13

Operations no instruction computes are Vun: `lang.float.{Bits, bits, from_bits, fraction_bits}`
expose the encoding, `lang.float.soft.{rem, mul_add}` compute over it, and `#[lang.lower.by(f)]` on
a primitive makes each run-time call of it a call of `f` when its caller is made concrete. A call of
a primitive whose parameters are all `$` ones is known when compiling (typed at compile time, so an
integer has the type of its value alone); a `$` binder is a value, so borrowing it borrows a copy of
that type; `.wrap` into a range of any power-of-two size, 2^127 and 2^128 included
