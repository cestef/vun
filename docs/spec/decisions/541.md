`Iter.peekable()` gives `Peekable(T, F)`, a linear value holding the frame and the one item looked
at: `peek` borrows it, `next` takes it, `end` gives the frame up. `rev` is a method of slices
(`xs.rev()` yields borrows from the last), not of every frame, which cannot be read backwards
without a buffer
