# 540. `Iter.sum(A)` adds the items of a frame into a total of the type `A` the caller

Status: accepted
Where: 04, 06

`Iter.sum(A)` adds the items of a frame into a total of the type `A` the caller names (`A` any
`Int(r)`, from `std.int.Integer`): a sum has no range of its own, since every addition widens it, so
the caller says where the total must lie and each step is a compound assignment, a trap site when
the total leaves `A` (spec 04, rule 9). Floats are folded (`fold(f64, 0.0, ...)`)
