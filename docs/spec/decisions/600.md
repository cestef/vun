# 600. `vun bench` takes samples

Status: accepted
Where: 11

`vun bench` takes samples (spec 11, section 5): `--samples n` (10) runs of the program's measured
benchmarks, each run one sample per benchmark of its time per iteration, so what a process does
(placement, start, the clock) is in the sample and not only its loop. The statistics (`Statistics`)
are the median, which no sample moves alone, the median absolute deviation as the spread, and the
mean and range of the samples inside Tukey's fences (1.5 interquartile ranges), the others counted
as outliers. The runner and its protocol are as decision 384 left them; the host aggregates
