# 346. The members a spread brings into a `struct` or `enum` body are members of its

Status: accepted
Where: 01

The members a spread brings into a `struct` or `enum` body are members of its type, reached through
it as declared ones are (`lang.arch.x86_64.names.mov`, `names.rax`), each where it is declared; a
spread naming no namespace leaves the type's members incomplete. A spread's target and the names it
brings are read once for namespaces and type bodies alike
