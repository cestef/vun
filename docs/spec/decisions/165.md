# 165. A namespace's link symbol names are the members of its `symbols` struct, beside

Status: accepted
Where: 08
Note: the user's choice

A namespace's link symbol names are the members of its `symbols` struct, beside the declarations
naming them: `lang.panic.symbols.trap`, `std.sync.symbols.{enter, leave}`, `std.task.symbols.sleep`
(replacing `trap_symbol`, `enter_symbol`, ...)
