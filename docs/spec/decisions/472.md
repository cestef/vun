# 472. A `$for` over a value that has no items to step through

Status: accepted

A `$for` over a value that has no items to step through (a number, a record, a value of a type with
its own iteration) is reported, `types.no_items`, where typing finds it; it was left to lowering,
which stopped without saying why. Its items are those of an array, a tuple or a range (spec 03,
section 1)
