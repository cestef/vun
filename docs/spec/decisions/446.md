# 446. A `$for` steps through the items of a tuple, `.{ 1, 2, 3 }` or `.{ u8, u16 }`

Status: accepted
Where: 03

A `$for` steps through the items of a tuple, `.{ 1, 2, 3 }` or `.{ u8, u16 }`, as through an array's
(`Value::items`), each step typed by its own item, so the items may be of different types
