# 319. Each node kind is declared by its production in `grammar.vun`

Status: accepted
Where: 13

Each node kind is declared by its production in `grammar.vun` (`.binding(.optional(.\mut), .pattern,
":=", .value)`: tokens by spelling or kind, words, fields, `optional`, `many`, `separated`,
`choice`, sequences), rather than by its slots alone; the slots and their counts are derived from it
(a field under `many` or `separated` holds many, under `optional` or `choice` one at most), a node
is anchored at its production's first token, and the CST verifier checks that every node recovery
did not build spells its kind's production (`NodeKind::spelled_by`, matched by sets of positions so
no input backtracks), over every tree the parser builds, the fuzzer's included. The productions are
what editor grammars are generated from
