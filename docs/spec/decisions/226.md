# 226. Lowering merges a block into its only predecessor ending in a jump to it

Status: accepted
Where: 13

Lowering merges a block into its only predecessor ending in a jump to it, inlines small functions
(internal, Vun's convention, an entry returning after at most eight instructions; instructions keep
their origins, so line tables still name the callee) and simplifies again; the native backend emits
nothing for a function no export, address taken or call reaches (`vun_lir::analysis::Reached`). The
backend test programs lose a further 6 to 22 percent of their instructions (`floats.vun` gains 2)
