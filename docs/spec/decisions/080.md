# 80. Loops are typed

Status: accepted
Where: 01, appendix B
Note: on the agent's recommendation

Loops are typed: `break v` values join into the loop's value, a loop ending by its head adds `()`;
`for p in e` finds its items through the table `iteration` (the witness of `lang.ops.Iterate`, its
`Iter` coroutine interface, the payload of `yielded` in what `resume` returns); `lang.ops` holds
compiler-provided impls of `Iterate` for `Bounds`, `mem.Slice` and `mem.Array`; iterating a slice
through a borrow yields borrows of its items (two corpus programs now read `b.*`); `.*` reads
through a borrow or a pointer
