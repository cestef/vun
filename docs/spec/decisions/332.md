# 332. `$for` and `$if` in an array or tuple literal generate its items

Status: accepted
Where: 03

`$for` and `$if` in an array or tuple literal generate its items: typing types each step's items
under its binding and knows the length once the items are, MIR builds them per step (the one
`unroll` over any list), and compile-time evaluation and member tables share `Env::generated`
