# 441. Verifying ahead keeps what it finds of a memo reaching, through its own reads

Status: accepted
Where: 13

Verifying ahead keeps what it finds of a memo reaching, through its own reads, a memo being looked
at above it (`Found::Cyclic`), as it keeps `Fails` and `Holds`: the answer is the same from any memo
reaching it, verifying in order tells, and a memo of a cycle is looked at once in a revision
(`Event::Looked`, counted in `--memos`) instead of once per path reaching it, which was exponential
in the cycle's width. Only a memo another thread is looking at (`Found::Unsure`) is looked at again.
Declaring an interface by a call stays discouraged (402), but no longer hangs
