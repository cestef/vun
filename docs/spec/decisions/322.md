# 322. A declaration's origin is identified by its name inside the declaration around

Status: accepted
Where: 10

A declaration's origin is identified by its name inside the declaration around it and by `nth`, how
many declared before it there share that name, so two locals of one name in sibling blocks are two
origins; `OriginTable::declaration` refuses two declarations of one identity in one text, since
persisted origins are pooled by identity and one would be read back as the other (a reused `mir`
memo of `std/os/linux/kernel/tls.vun` differed from its recomputation)
