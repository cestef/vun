Status: accepted

Checking a program lints the files of its own package, not those of the dependencies it reaches
(type errors, syntax errors and name errors in a dependency are still reported). A dependency's
lints are its owner's to see, by checking that package; the cold check of `hello` spent 41% of its
instructions linting the 22 files of `lang` and `std` it reaches.
