Status: accepted
Where: 08

`std.os.entropy(bytes)` fills bytes from the system's source for secrets, a symbol each runtime
exports (`getrandom`, `getentropy`, `random_get`; Windows `unsupported` until `ProcessPrng` is
linked). `std.crypto` holds SHA-256 (`Hasher`), HMAC-SHA-256, ChaCha20 and a constant-time `equal`,
checked against the published vectors, and `SecureRng`, ChaCha20 keyed from the system with fast key
erasure and a mix of fresh entropy every 1 MiB. Also `std.time.date` (proleptic Gregorian dates and
UTC moments from Unix time, ISO 8601), `std.net` (IPv4 and IPv6, RFC 5952 text) and `std.uuid`
(versions 4 and 7), and `<` on slices and arrays (words order)
