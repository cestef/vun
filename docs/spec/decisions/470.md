Status: accepted

A member of the witness an impl declares that leaves its annotation out binds what the requirement
of its interface says it is for `std.lints.naming.case`: `Error := ();` in an impl of `Writer` is a
type, as the member's function types are taken from the requirement (decision 404). `Env::annotated`
gives where the requirement is written, `asked` its function types
