# 394. Derived impls reach tuples

Status: accepted
Where: 12
Note: the user's approval

Derived impls reach tuples (the unit among them) and records, whose parts are their items, as
structs and enums; `Option` and `Result` are equal by derivation, not by impls of their own.
`std.io` holds `Reader` (`read`, `read_exact`, the requirement `ended`), `Bytes`, a reader of a
slice, and `Writer` as `std.fmt.Writer`; `std.os.Stream` is a `Reader`, its `read` now that of its
impl; `std.text.Slab(n)` keeps up to `n` bytes inline and is a `Writer`
