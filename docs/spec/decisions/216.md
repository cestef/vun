# 216. `vun build` on a package's `build.vun` carries out its plan

Status: accepted
Where: 12
Note: the user's choice

`vun build` on a package's `build.vun` carries out its plan: actions are read as
`vun_sema::plan::Action` through the handles of `std.build`; a program's options are the typed
record `std.build.Options` (`root`, `target: lang.target.Spec`, `deps: &[Use]`, `memory: &[Region]`,
the last two empty by default) instead of a record kept as `lang.meta.Any`; each program is built in
a session of its own (`Choice::Given` the target read), its memory regions into `LinkPlan.regions`;
`flash`, `test_on`, dependency parameters and lint levels are recorded but not carried out yet,
warnings `plan.unsupported`; a step that does not read as an action is `plan.unread`
