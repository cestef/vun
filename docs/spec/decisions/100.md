# 100. `.` looks through owners as through borrows

Status: accepted
Where: 01, 05
Note: the user accepted the review's proposals

`.` looks through owners as through borrows: when a struct with a `#[kind.owns]` field lacks a
member (in its namespace and impls), lookup continues to its target (`Env::reach`, one rule for
typing, places and receivers), so `node.value` works on `Own(Node)` and `&Own(Node)`. Moving a
linear value out through a borrow's `.*` is `place.move_out_of_borrow` (typed along the path; an
owner held by value may be moved out of)
